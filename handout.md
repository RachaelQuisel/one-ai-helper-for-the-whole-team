### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **The bot is a role, not a task.** A job title and a job description you can evaluate.
2. **Shared brain, private chats.** It knows the business; it doesn't gossip.
3. **Drafts first, always.** The bot proposes; a human disposes. Nothing sends without your tap.
